Publications

Nickell Meets Stambaugh: A Tale of Two Biases in Panel Predictive Regressions

with Ziwei Mei and Zhentao Shi

Accepted at Journal of Econometrics, 2026

We study the coexistence of Nickell and Stambaugh biases in panel predictive regressions and develop a novel estimator to effectively remove this composite bias.

In panel predictive regressions with persistent covariates, coexistence of the Nickell bias and the Stambaugh bias imposes challenges for estimation and hypothesis testing. This paper introduces an innovative estimator, the Double IVX (DIVX), inspired by the IVX technique in time series. DIVX effectively removes this composite Nickell-Stambaugh bias and reinstates standard inferential procedures based on the t-statistic. This new procedure achieves unified inference across a wide range of modes of persistence in panel predictive regressions when the cross-sectional dimension and the time dimension are comparably large. Such desirable properties were unattainable by existing methods, including the popular within-group estimator. Extensive Monte Carlo simulations demonstrate the robustness of DIVX under a variety of settings. We apply DIVX to panel data of financial markets in developed economies to examine the predictability of stock returns.

A Relaxation Approach to Synthetic Control

with Zhentao Shi and Yapeng Zheng

R&R at Econometric Theory, 2026

We develop a relaxation approach to synthetic control when there are many control units.

The synthetic control method (SCM) is widely used for constructing the counterfactual of a treated unit based on data from control units in a donor pool. Allowing the donor pool contains more control units than time periods, we propose a novel machine learning algorithm, named SCM-relaxation, for counterfactual prediction. Our relaxation approach minimizes an information-theoretic measure of the weights subject to a set of relaxed linear inequality constraints in addition to the simplex constraint. When the donor pool exhibits a group structure, SCM-relaxation approximates the equal weights within each group to diversify the prediction risk. Asymptotically, the proposed estimator achieves oracle performance in terms of out-of-sample prediction accuracy. We demonstrate our method by Monte Carlo simulations and by an empirical application that assesses the economic impact of Brexit on the United Kingdom's real GDP.

Cointegrated Regressors: Where Folded Concave Penalties Go Beyond LASSO

with Hongqi Chen, Ziwei Mei, and Zhentao Shi

Working paper, 2026

We use folded concave penalties to improve variable selection and prediction in high-dimensional regressions with cointegrated regressors.

In high-dimensional predictive regressions, LASSO can shrink the coefficients of cointegrated regressors too aggressively to estimate them consistently. We study folded concave penalties, including SCAD and MCP, as an alternative. We establish a local oracle property for penalized regression and a global oracle property when the regression is subject to a penalty-level constraint. The constrained formulation yields a global solution with consistent coefficient estimates and oracle prediction performance. Simulations and an application forecasting euro area unemployment show improvements over LASSO in variable selection and out-of-sample prediction.
